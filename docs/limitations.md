# Limitations

- The census profile is from 2016 and is not a current population estimate.
- Only age-related demographic variables are present. The source cannot support a multidimensional socioeconomic index using income, housing, language, immigration, education, employment, or Indigenous identity.
- The vulnerability score is an age-structure proxy and scenario tool, not a validated prediction of healthcare need.
- GNR measures census data uncertainty; it is not socioeconomic vulnerability.
- The ODHF release is an open compilation with irregular update frequency and uneven source coverage.
- Facility presence and type do not reveal ownership, VCH responsibility, staffing, beds, hours, service scope, throughput, quality, accessibility, or capacity.
- The project does not have actual VCH staffing, operational budgets, utilization, wait times, patient flows, outcomes, or unmet-need data.
- Whole-CHSA population is used for CHSA 3242 despite 84.6% city overlap. Areal population apportionment would be methodologically weak without smaller-area population data.
- Strathcona has no separate row/polygon in the supplied 2016 CHSA data. The custom CHA 2 business rule cannot be fully quantified from this source.
- Sensitivity analysis shows scenario rankings change under alternative weights. Decisions require stakeholder review and richer operational evidence.
- No causal inference or statistical significance is claimed. Correlations are descriptive only.
